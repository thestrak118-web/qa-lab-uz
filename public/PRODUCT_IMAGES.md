# Product photography

The QA Lab demo storefront bundles 39 distinct photographs locally under `public/products/`, one for each stable product ID. `src/lib/productImages.js` maps the IDs so historical exercise snapshots use the same product-specific images without a data migration.
They illustrate fictional practice products; product names, stock, prices and reviews are seeded training data. No photographer or pictured brand endorses this application. This application does not sell the pictured products or the photos.

All photographs were downloaded from Pexels on 2026-10-08. Pexels licenses these images for free use on websites/apps, including commercial use. Attribution is appreciated but not required. Source and license links are retained here for provenance.

The additional photographs were visually inspected together for category fit and duplicate subjects. They remain illustrative photographs of fictional training products; specifications are demo data. Regression tests verify that the catalog contains 39 distinct paths and 39 distinct image hashes.
